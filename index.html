TRACKER POP-UP GUIDE

Your complete guide to using the RAN Boss Tracker.

1

CLICK KILLED NOW
TO START THE TIMER

After clicking Killed Now, the timer will start counting down to the next spawn.

2

CLICK THE CALENDAR ICON
TO EDIT THE TIME

Click the calendar icon to choose a new date & time.

3

THEN CLICK
SET MANUAL

After setting the date & time, click Set Manual to save the time to the tracker.

4

WATCH THE NEXT SPAWN
LIVE TRACKING PANEL

The big panel at the top always shows the soonest boss — its location, channel and a live countdown. Use the left menu (Home, Boss List, Kill History, Settings) to jump around.

5

READ THE CHANNEL COLORS

Every card and the Next Spawn panel carry a colored channel badge, matching the legend in the left menu:

CH 0 CH 1 CH 2 CH 3
6

USE SMART SEARCH
WITH SHORTCUTS

Type shortcuts in the Boss search box and separate them with spaces — every word must match. Example: ch0 ef shows only CH 0 Etherial Fist.

Channel: ch0 ch1 ch2 ch3
Boss: dsjr ef nk ds dam cj
Map: mpcamp sgcamp phcamp mph sgh phh py leo

7

ALL BOSSES FILTER
BY RESPAWN OR CHANNEL

The tracker starts empty. Open the filter dropdown (or click Settings in the left menu) and tap the boss names (or a spawn time like 1H) you want to track, then tap CH 0–3 to narrow by channel (none picked = all channels). Show All / Hide All for quick changes. Your setup is remembered.

8

AUTO SORT &
ALARM TOGGLES

Auto Sort keeps the soonest-spawning bosses at the top automatically. Alarm plays a sound when a boss is about to spawn.

9

DRAG TO TRASH OR
RESET ALL

Drag a card's ⠿ grip onto the trash bin in the left menu to reset just that boss. Click Reset All if the server restarts to reset every timer at once.

10

LOST THE TIMER?
SEARCH KILL HISTORY

Kill History shows each boss's icon, location, channel and exact kill time. Use its search box (e.g. dsjr, sg campus, ch1) to find the latest kill, then edit it into your tracker.

11

TO CLEAR HISTORY
INPUT THE ADMIN PIN

Enter your Admin PIN then click Clear to clear the Kill History.

12

CLICK & DRAG TO
PAN THE BOSS GRID

On a small browser window, click and hold anywhere on the boss grid then drag to scroll. On touchscreens, just swipe sideways.

13

FILTER A BOSS TO SEE
ITS MAP LOCATION

Search or filter a boss (e.g. dsjr) and the Map Location panel in the left menu shows the boss icon on every spawn spot of that map. Click the map to enlarge it.
